PolicySetu

Bridging Government Policies & People

Democratizing access to Indian central, state, and union territory policies through transparent, verified civic intelligence.

Verified Official Portals
PolicySetu ingests data strictly from verified official government websites, including PIB, India.gov.in, MyGov, state gazettes, and parliamentary repositories. We never fabricate policies or source URLs.
RAG-Grounded AI Analysis
Complex circulars and gazettes are translated into accessible language using Retrieval-Augmented Generation. Every answer provided by PolicySetu AI links back to exact clauses and official files.
Amendment Tracking
When gazette provisions change, PolicySetu’s cryptographic hash pipelines detect changes, generating side-by-side diffs and impact assessments for farmers, students, and entrepreneurs.
Privacy by Design
No phone numbers required, no SMS spam, no OTP verification. PolicySetu is a pure civic-tech discovery tool focused entirely on public transparency and citizen enlightenment.

Join Us in Transforming Civic Awareness

Whether you are an agriculturalist navigating PM-KISAN, a student evaluating educational reforms under NEP 2020, or a startup founder tracking tax exemptions, PolicySetu is your civic bridge.
