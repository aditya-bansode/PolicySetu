PolicySetu Truth in Monitoring Guarantee

PolicySetu strictly enforces the rule that no government source may be displayed as monitored unless an automated adapter has been authored, verified, and successfully executed. Candidate sources that rely on client-side SPA rendering (such as India Code DSpace 7) or stateful ASP.NET session tokens (such as e-Gazette) are explicitly reported as Inaccessible until official headless or authenticated drivers are established.
